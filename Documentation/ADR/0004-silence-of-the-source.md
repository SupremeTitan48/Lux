# ADR 0004: When the source is silent

Date: 2026-10-13. Status: accepted.

## Decision

When the 1662 book (as supplied) gives no rule:

- If the question is which of two printed texts to say (a holy day on a
  Sunday), offer both as `alternatives`, in a fixed order, with a diagnostic.
- If a choice is needed to proceed and one reading is customary (the
  collect between the Epiphany and its first Sunday; which omitted Epiphany
  Sundays fill surplus Sundays after Trinity), make it and state it in a
  diagnostic.
- If text is missing (the text of a lesson), show a placeholder and a
  warning. Never substitute the ordinary course or other text. The same
  holds when a table appoints nothing (the Kalendar's blank for St Matthias
  on 24 February in a leap year, when the feast is kept on the 25th).

## Consequences

The office is always usable and never silently wrong. Diagnostics give the
reviewer a list of every editorial decision taken on a given day.
